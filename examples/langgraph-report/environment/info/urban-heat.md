# Urban heat islands — source notes

Cities run warmer than surrounding countryside because dark surfaces absorb
solar radiation and dense construction traps it. The effect is strongest at
night, when rural areas cool and urban cores do not.

## Magnitude

Typical city centres run 4 to 7 degrees Celsius warmer than nearby rural land on
clear summer nights.

## Mitigation

Reflective roofing, street trees, and permeable surfaces each reduce peak
surface temperature. Tree canopy is the most durable intervention because it
provides shade and evapotranspiration together.
